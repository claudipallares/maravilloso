# Maravilloso Videojuego

A tiny pixel-art browser game starring **Pixel Valdeande**.

Move the master left and right, catch the falling **BTC**, **ETH** and **VM** coins, and dodge the **phishing hooks**. As you fill your wallet, night turns into day over the Roque Nublo.

**Play it:** [https://YOUR-USER.github.io/maravilloso-videojuego/](https://claudipallares.github.io/maravilloso/)

## Goal
Collect at least:
- **1 BTC** (each coin = 0.1)
- **10 ETH** (each coin = 1)
- **1000 VM** (each coin = 10)

You have 3 lives. Every phishing hook you touch costs one. Things fall faster and faster.

## Controls
- **Mobile:** drag your finger anywhere, or tap the left/right half of the screen
- **PC:** arrow keys, `M` to mute

## Tech
One single `index.html` with HTML5 `<canvas>` and plain JavaScript. No libraries, no build step.
Sprites are embedded in the file; music and sound effects are generated with the Web Audio API.
